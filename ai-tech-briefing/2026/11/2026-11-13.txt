INDIA: AI & TECH

1. Shield AI opens Bengaluru engineering office for autonomy software
Shield AI announced that it opened an engineering office at GoodWorks GCC Nexus in Bengaluru, where engineers will develop and test autonomy software for Indian aircraft and defence systems. The team will work on Hivemind, its autonomy platform, and the company plans to hire in autonomy, flight software, simulation, systems integration and testing. The office is an engineering and hiring expansion, not a new Indian procurement announcement; its significance is the local software and integration capability it could build alongside existing partnerships.
Source: https://shield.ai/shield-opens-bengaluru-engineering-office-to-build-autonomy-in-india-for-india/

2. NALCO targets November commissioning for India's first gallium recovery pilot
National Aluminium Company (NALCO) said its gallium-recovery pilot at Damanjodi in Odisha is nearly ready and is targeted to start this month. The project is being developed with BARC and the Heavy Water Board to recover gallium from the refinery's Bayer liquor; gallium compounds are used in semiconductors, advanced electronics and telecom equipment. This is a pilot, not a commercial-scale plant, and its recovery yield and scalability remain to be demonstrated.
Source: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/nalco-to-commission-india-s-first-gallium-recovery-plant-this-month-cmd-126111301331_1.html

3. BYD's Denza luxury EV brand debuts in India with Z9 GT and D9
BYD's luxury brand Denza made its India debut with the Z9 GT electric performance car and D9 electric MPV, priced at ₹1.56 crore and ₹90 lakh ex-showroom respectively. The Z9 GT uses a tri-motor all-wheel-drive system rated at 850 kW and a 122.49 kWh battery; BYD claims up to 670 km range under the NEDC test cycle. The launch expands competition in the premium EV segment, but range and performance figures are manufacturer claims and NEDC figures should not be treated as real-world range.
Source: https://auto.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/passenger-vehicle/byds-luxury-brand-denza-enters-india-with-z9-gt-d9-prices-start-at-90-lakh/135801048

4. Paytm receives in-principle RBI authorisation for prepaid payment instruments
One97 Communications disclosed that its wholly owned subsidiary Paytm Payments Services received an RBI letter dated 13 November granting in-principle authorisation to set up and operate a prepaid payment instrument system. This is a regulatory step toward re-entering the wallet business, but it is not the final Certificate of Authorisation; the company says it can offer prepaid instruments such as wallets only after final authorisation. The distinction matters because the announcement does not mean Paytm wallets are immediately available.
Source: https://paytm.com/document/ir/stock-exchange-submissions/fy2026-27/nov/Disclosure_Reg_30_Listing_Regulations_13_Nov_2026.pdf

5. Karnataka backs Kalaburagi startup hub under the LEAP initiative
Karnataka said it will develop Kalaburagi as a regional startup hub under its Local Ecosystem Acceleration Programme, with a Centre of Excellence for rural and agri-focused startups allocated a grant of about ₹12.5 crore. Plans also include LEAP Labs co-working spaces and an IT Park, while the Kalyana Karnataka Startup Dialogue scheduled for 14 November is intended to connect founders with investors and industry. The initiative aims to spread startup activity beyond Bengaluru, but the planned facilities and ecosystem outcomes will need to be tracked as they move from announcement to operation.
Source: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/hubballi/kalaburagi-to-emerge-regional-startup-hub-under-leap-initiative/articleshow/135811734.cms

6. Bengaluru inaugurates first operational stretch of Namma Metro's Pink Line
The 7.5 km elevated stretch between Kalena Agrahara and Tavarekere was inaugurated on 13 November, adding six stations to Bengaluru's network; passenger services were scheduled to begin on 14 November. JICA says its development loan supports signalling and train-control systems, rolling stock and consulting services for the project. The full 21.26 km corridor, including the underground section to Nagawara, is still incomplete, so this is a phased opening rather than the entire Pink Line entering service.

GLOBAL AI & TECHNOLOGY

7. Microsoft restores services after a prolonged Microsoft 365 outage
A global outage disrupted Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and Copilot, with recovery extending through 13 November after the incident began late on 12 November IST. Microsoft said the problem was linked to an issue introduced during an earlier network-capacity upgrade that surfaced after a later network change, and said it had no indication of a cyberattack or AI-agent cause. The incident showed how a failure in shared cloud infrastructure can affect both core workplace tools and AI integrations that depend on them; Microsoft said a fuller post-incident review would follow.

https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/13/microsoft-outage-outlook-teams-word-excel-reports
https://www.techradar.com/news/live/outlook-outage-nov-2026

8. Cerebras reports record Q3 revenue, raises outlook and signs Microsoft cloud deal
Cerebras reported third-quarter GAAP revenue of $210.8 million, up 55% year over year, and said cloud and services revenue reached $141.9 million, up 265%. It raised its 2026 core revenue outlook to $940–960 million and said it had signed a cloud deal with Microsoft, while its results release also described a new top-three hyperscaler deal and 700 gross megawatts of data-centre capacity secured for delivery by the end of 2027. The capacity and revenue targets are company disclosures, and the Microsoft deal's details are due later; the announcement nonetheless shows demand for alternatives to Nvidia in high-speed inference.

9. SMIC expects AI chip supply constraints to persist through 2027
Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corp (SMIC) co-CEO Zhao Haijun said strong demand from data centres and private clouds is likely to keep the AI semiconductor supply chain tight through 2027. He said customers still face shortages and that some equipment orders have lead times exceeding 24 months, while memory supply and demand may not balance until mid-to-late 2028. The comments point to constraints beyond chip design itself, including manufacturing capacity and specialised equipment.
Source: https://www.reuters.com/technology/smic-says-ai-chip-supply-chain-will-remain-tight-through-2027-2026-11-13/

10. Reuters review finds more than $3 billion in Chinese AI-compute projects specifying Nvidia chips
A Reuters review of more than 150 procurement notices, investment plans, contracts and disclosures identified Chinese AI-computing projects worth over 23 billion yuan, or about $3.2 billion, that specify Nvidia processors. The total includes planned and signed projects, completed installations and supporting infrastructure, not simply chip purchases; some projects have uncertain supply or delivery and may require redesigns using domestic processors. The review is evidence of continuing demand for Nvidia hardware in China, but it is not proof that every listed GPU has been delivered or that each project violates export controls.
Source: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/huang-exit-us-scrutiny-imperil-3-billion-chinese-ai-projects-reliant-nvidia-2026-11-13/

11. US export-control dispute over Nvidia-linked Megaspeed intensifies
US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Treasury, the Pentagon, State Department and US Trade Representative had supported adding Singapore-based Megaspeed to the Commerce Department's Entity List for months, while Commerce controlled the decision. The remarks intensified scrutiny of how Washington reviews advanced-chip sales and possible diversion to China, with lawmakers pressing Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick for explanations. No new Entity List designation was announced, and allegations around Megaspeed remain allegations rather than a public finding of wrongdoing.
Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/bessent-says-treasury-favored-limits-megaspeed-2026-11-13/

12. South Korea says Nvidia's 260,000-GPU commitment remains in place
South Korea's Science and ICT Minister Bae Kyung-hoon said Nvidia had confirmed it would honour its previously announced pledge to supply 260,000 advanced GPUs to the country. The commitment dates to the 2025 APEC meetings, but the 13 November statement reaffirmed it and also pointed to continued work on integrating Korean AI chips with Nvidia's platform, building compute infrastructure and advancing physical-AI research and talent. The update matters for South Korea's AI-compute plans, though the original pledge is not a new order announced this week.
Source: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-says-nvidia-will-keep-gpu-pledges-after-huangs-resignation-2026-11-13/

13. Nscale's amended IPO filing shows rapid growth alongside heavy losses and debt
Nvidia-backed AI infrastructure provider Nscale filed an amended US IPO registration statement dated 12 November US time, which reached the SEC in the early hours of 13 November IST. The filing estimated nine-month revenue of $307.5 million, compared with $21.3 million a year earlier, but also an estimated net loss of $1.69 billion and total debt of $5.52 billion as of 30 September. These are preliminary estimates in an IPO document, not audited full-year results; they underline the capital intensity and financing risk behind rapid AI data-centre expansion.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm

14. Anthropic's IPO order book reaches about six-times oversubscription
Reuters reported that by the end of Thursday in the United States, orders for Anthropic's planned IPO were about six times the shares available, equivalent to early Friday in India. The report cited people familiar with the book and said existing backers may receive priority allocations; the offering seeks up to $64.4 billion and could imply a fully diluted valuation of about $1.4 trillion. Demand can change during the roadshow, so the reported coverage is not a final allocation or pricing result.
Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/investors-flock-anthropic-ipo-question-durability-ai-lead-2026-11-13/

15. LangChain raises $300 million at a $3 billion valuation
LangChain announced a $300 million Series C led by existing investor CapitalG, with new participation from GIC and DFJ Growth and support from existing investors. The company said the funding will advance LangSmith, its platform for running, evaluating, observing and governing AI agents, and highlighted more than 7,000 organisations using the product. The round is a significant bet on the tooling layer around production agents, but adoption and customer productivity claims remain company-reported.
Source: https://www.langchain.com/blog/series-c

16. OpenAI introduces ChatGPT Wallet for user-approved agent purchases
OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, allowing agents to make purchases using payment tokens rather than seeing a user's actual card details. Users review the items and total and approve each transaction with a security check such as Face ID or fingerprint; the initial rollout is limited to ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 or older in the United States with eligible US-issued cards, through Dots. The release is an early move toward agentic commerce, but merchant compatibility, fraud controls and the boundaries of delegated purchasing will determine how useful it becomes.
Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/

17. Uber confirms driver-licence data exposure through Fireworks AI vendor breach
Uber said a third-party provider notified it on 9 October that attackers had accessed internal credentials and used them to reach some Uber data; TechCrunch identified the provider as Fireworks AI on 13 November. Breach notices reviewed by TechCrunch listed stolen driver-licence information for people in Massachusetts, South Carolina and Rhode Island, with further notifications possible, while Uber did not provide a total affected count. The intrusion occurred in October, so the 13 November development is the public identification of the vendor and the scope of exposed data, not the date of the breach itself.
Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/uber-confirms-hackers-stole-thousands-of-drivers-license-information-in-data-breach/

18. EU leaders hold their first dedicated strategic discussion on artificial intelligence
At an informal European Council meeting in Dublin on 13 November, EU leaders held their first in-depth discussion focused specifically on AI, joined by Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch and Nobel laureate Geoffrey Hinton. The agenda covered European competitiveness and AI sovereignty, infrastructure and clean energy, reducing supply-chain dependencies, adoption across business and government, and the effects of AI on rights, labour markets, safety and security. The meeting produced a strategic discussion rather than a new binding law or funding commitment, but it signals that AI capacity and governance are now central to the EU leaders' agenda.
